# Stage 02 — Plan

> **ICM Layer 4: Context.** Your role for this stage. Read `../CLAUDE.md` first.

**Job:** decide what to actually make this week — matched to the user's cadence
and to what's been working.

## Input
- **`../01_capture/output/ideas.md`** — the captured ideas from Stage 01.

## Read
- `../profile.md` — publishing cadence and the user's real constraint (time / ideas / finishing).
- `../memory/what-worked.md` — lean the week toward proven patterns.
- `../memory/published.md` (last ~10 entries) — avoid repeats, spot follow-ups.

## Do
Produce a week plan:

```
## Week of <date> — plan
**Anchor piece:** <the one substantial thing> — pillar, angle, format.
  Why this one: <ties to what-worked or a timely reason>
**Supporting pieces:** <list, sized to cadence>
  - <format>: <angle>
**The one that matters:** if you only finish one thing, finish <X>. Because <…>
```

Rules:
- **Match the cadence, then cut 20%.** Solos overcommit. If the constraint is
  "finishing," plan *less*. A plan that gets finished beats one that gets abandoned.
- Bias toward **anchor + repurpose**: make one good thing, spin it into many.
- Every piece maps to a pillar. Flag anything that doesn't — it's a new pillar or a distraction.

## Output
Write the plan to **`output/week-plan.md`**. This is the input to Stage 03.

## Close the pass
1. Append to `../memory/session-log.md`: `<date> — 02_plan — week planned, anchor: <title>`.
2. Tell the human the plan and that the next stage is **03_create** (start with the anchor). Stop for review.
